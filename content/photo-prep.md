For the highest gallery resolution, upload your processed JPEG, PNG, TIFF or WebP at its original dimensions directly to Pages CMS or the connected Drive folder. The gallery creates responsive previews and keeps a full-resolution viewing copy.

This optional tool creates smaller WebP copies without the source file’s EXIF metadata. Use it when you want a smaller upload; it reduces resolution. Processing happens in your browser. Your original files stay on your device.

Confirm permission to publish, check that no sensitive details are visible, and keep a separate copy of your originals. Add photo descriptions, captions, and photographer credit in Pages CMS after uploading.
