Resize images before uploading them to Pages CMS. This tool creates web-friendly WebP copies without the source file’s EXIF metadata. Processing happens in your browser. Your original files stay on your device.

Confirm permission to publish, check that no sensitive details are visible, and keep a separate copy of your originals. Add photo descriptions, captions, and photographer credit in Pages CMS after uploading.
